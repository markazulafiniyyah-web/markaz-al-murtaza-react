Common questions

Choosing an online Quran institute

Can beginners join the online Quran classes?

Yes. Lessons can begin with Arabic letters and Noorani Qaida before progressing to fluent Nazra Quran reading.

Are classes available for both kids and adults?

Yes. The learning pace and lesson plan are adapted to the student's age, experience and goals.

Can I study Hifz or Tajweed online?

Yes. Separate one-to-one programs are available for Hifz memorization, practical Tajweed and Nazra Quran reading.
